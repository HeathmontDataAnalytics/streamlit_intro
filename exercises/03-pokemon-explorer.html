So far your apps have shown the same thing every time. Now you'll add widgets: checkboxes, drop-down menus and sliders that let the person using the app choose what to see.

You'll build a Pokémon explorer. The data, data/pokemon.csv, has one row for each Pokémon (including Mega and other special forms), with its types, its battle stats and its generation. It comes from Pokemon Stats by Ulrik Thyge Pedersen on Kaggle (licence: CC BY 4.0), with a few errors corrected.

In this exercise you will:

  • add a checkbox, a selectbox, a slider and a multiselect
  • use what the user picks to filter the data
  • let the user choose what a chart shows
  • lay out the app with a sidebar and columns

The big idea: every time someone changes a widget, Streamlit runs your whole script again from the top. Step 1 explains how this works.
